What to share about your setting

Belize’s cayes and mainland are not interchangeable for dispatch or travel. The most useful information is a named accommodation, exact entrance, permission for a visiting therapist and a suitable private treatment room.

Choosing a treatment

Guests may enquire about Swedish relaxation massage, deep tissue massage or couples massage. A two-person session requires two professionals and suitable space; none is guaranteed by submitting a form.

Before you commit

Confirm therapist qualifications, local availability, realistic travel and the full price. These are enquiries, not published coverage guarantees. If your hotel has an in-house spa or restricts outside providers, its rules take priority.

BELIZE · SAN PEDRO / AMBERGRIS CAYE

Plan your private massage in San Pedro / Ambergris Caye

San Pedro is on Ambergris Caye, not on the Belize mainland. A hotel in town, a villa north of the bridge and a remote beachfront residence can have different transfer options and access rules.

Where the visit takes place

Golf-cart routes, private docks and resort security matter when moving massage equipment on the island. Share the property's exact name, whether a road or boat is needed and whether visiting providers are authorised.

Suitable service requests

Private relaxation, deep tissue and couples treatments may be requested, but a suitable local therapist must be confirmed on Ambergris Caye. Couples or group sessions need enough private room and the right team.

Travel and timing

Travelling from Belize City to Ambergris Caye involves a separate flight or water transfer, so a mainland operator cannot be assumed to cover a San Pedro address without a specific arrangement.

San Pedro / Ambergris Caye questions

Can a massage professional come to a villa north of San Pedro?

Only after confirming the actual route, equipment transport, host permission and qualified provider on the caye.

Are transfers from mainland Belize included?

No. Water or air transfers must be assessed and explicitly reflected in the full quote.
